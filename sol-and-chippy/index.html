Adventures of Sol and Chippy

Two cats, twelve levels, and one long walk home.

Download for Android

Get the game as an app file and install it on your phone.

Download the game

app-solandchippy.apk, about 13 MB

How to install

  1. Tap the download button on your Android phone.
  2. Open the file when it finishes. Your phone may ask you to allow installs from your browser or Files app. Allow it for this one install.
  3. Tap Install, then open Adventures of Sol and Chippy.

Your phone shows this warning for any app that doesn't come from the Play Store.

About the game

You play two cats, Sol and Chippy, through twelve levels. They don't jump the same: Sol jumps higher, and Chippy's jump is shorter.

The game is still growing, so some levels may change as it gets updates.
